**What was done**

- **Noise per scene.** All 12 scenes are 3/3 in both runs, and every one of the 72 sessions gave its scene's verdict with the same check number. No scene's match rate moved and no verdict varied, so the threshold is 0.00. The record is `evals/baseline/linux-ubuntu-22.04/audit-noise.json`, written by `compare_audits.py --measure-noise … --out-record`.
- **`evals/compare_audits.py`.**
  - `--measure-noise` prints the per-scene report and writes the record. It refuses files that differ in commit, model, settings or runs per scenario, and refuses a partial file.
  - A comparison without `--noise` reads `audit-noise.json` beside its `--before` file. `--noise-record FILE` names another, `--noise-record none` uses none, and two `--noise` files given by hand still win.
  - A scene's bound is the larger of its own noise and the threshold. A non-zero difference no larger than the bound is judged «у межах шуму», or `NOT FIXED (у межах шуму)` for a must-fix scene, and fails nothing.
  - The header names the record used, or says `noise: NOT MEASURED`.
- **`evals/README.md`.** A new section, "The noise of the audit (board 058)", gives the threshold, how to measure it again, and what 0.00 does not prove.
- **`tests/test_audit_noise.py`.** 28 checks, added to `tests/fast-suites.txt`. They include the negative cases: two commits, a partial file, a single file, a named record that is absent, and the same one-session drop reading WORSE with no record. Two more checks tie the repository's record to its source files and to the threshold stated in the README.

**What the threshold does not show**

- With a threshold of 0.00, the «у межах шуму» label fires on no scene today. It is exercised only on synthetic result files in the suite.
- 72 sessions without a miss bound the per-session miss chance at about 4 % (95 %), not at zero. The README therefore says a single one-session difference is a reason to re-run that scene, not yet a finding.

**Decisions I made myself**

- I kept the measured threshold of 0.00 rather than adding a one-session floor. A floor would mean a 3/3 → 2/3 drop is never WORSE; without it, a false alarm costs one re-run of a scene.
- The two result files are full-tier records of `cdda02e`, so `engine.py release` will count one of them as the last full audit. `audit-noise.json` has no `tier` and is ignored by that check.
